# Start-day changes stretch one Meal Week

Changing the Household's start day must not move existing Meal Weeks, and Meal Weeks must never overlap or leave days uncovered. So the start day is stored as a schedule of changes, each effective from a date, rather than a single setting, and every Meal Week is derived from that schedule. A new start day takes effect on its first occurrence at least seven days after the latest existing (or current) Meal Week starts; that Meal Week stretches to meet it, so it is 8 to 13 days long, once.

## Considered Options

- **Switch immediately, allowing overlap**: simplest, but the current Meal Week's Meals would sit in a Meal Week that is no longer current, and Carry Over would offer nothing.
- **A short transition Meal Week covering the gap**: keeps every other Meal Week at seven days, but creates a 1–6 day Meal Week with a full Meal Count.

The stretch was chosen by the Household; it keeps planned Meals and the grocery rhythm intact through the change.
